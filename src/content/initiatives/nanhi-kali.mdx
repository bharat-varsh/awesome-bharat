**Every girl in school. Every girl learning. Every girl leading.**

Project Nanhi Kali, initiated by the KC Mahindra Education Trust in partnership with the Naandi Foundation, is India's largest and most comprehensive girl-child education program. The name "Nanhi Kali" means "little bud" — symbolizing the potential that every girl holds, waiting to bloom with the right support.

#### **What Makes Nanhi Kali Unique**

- **360-degree support model** — Unlike programs that only pay school fees, Nanhi Kali provides the complete package: school supplies (uniforms, books, bags), academic tutoring at community learning centres, and mentorship through Digital Learning Centres.
- **Digital Learning Centres** — In rural and tribal areas, Nanhi Kali runs technology-enabled after-school centres where girls access computer-aided learning, career guidance, and life-skills workshops.
- **Addressing root causes** — The program actively works with families and communities to change attitudes toward girl-child education — addressing child marriage, household chores, and early dropout.
- **Reach & scale** — 200,000+ girls supported annually across 14 states, with 90%+ retention rate and measurable improvements in academic performance and confidence.
- **Measurable outcomes** — Girls in the program show 95%+ attendance rates, significant improvement in math and language scores, and increased aspirations toward higher education and careers.

Project Nanhi Kali has been recognized globally for its holistic, scalable approach to girls' education. It proves that when you invest in a girl's education, you don't just change her life — you transform her entire community.
