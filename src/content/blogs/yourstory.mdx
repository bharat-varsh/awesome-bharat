**India's startup story, told one founder at a time.**

Founded in 2008 by Shradha Sharma, YourStory began as a simple blog to share stories of Indian entrepreneurs. Over 15 years, it has grown into the country's most comprehensive media platform covering the startup ecosystem — from early-stage ventures to unicorn IPOs.

#### **Why YourStory Matters**

- **The definitive startup archive** — Over 30,000+ stories covering Indian startups across sectors — fintech, healthtech, edtech, SaaS, D2C, and deep tech.
- **Founder-first narrative** — Every story puts the founder's journey front and center — the struggles, the breakthroughs, the failures, and the lessons learned.
- **Data & insights** — YourStory's research arm tracks funding rounds, acquisitions, and ecosystem trends, publishing monthly reports that VCs and founders rely on.
- **Community & events** — Through TechSparks (India's largest startup-tech festival) and regional meetups, YourStory connects the ecosystem offline.
- **Daily newsletter** — A crisp morning briefing that summarizes the day's most important startup news — free and widely read across the industry.

YourStory has documented India's startup journey from its infancy to the world's third-largest startup ecosystem. It is an indispensable resource for anyone who wants to understand — or participate in — India's entrepreneurial revolution.
