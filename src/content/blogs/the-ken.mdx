**The story behind the story — beyond the headlines.**

Founded in 2016 by Seema Singh, The Ken is a Bangalore-based business journalism publication that refuses to chase the news cycle. Instead, it focuses on _explanatory, investigative, and narrative longform stories_ that help you understand not just what happened, but why it matters.

#### **What Makes The Ken Essential Reading**

- **Deeply reported analysis** — Stories take weeks to report, with multiple sources, data analysis, and on-ground verification. No press releases, no PR fluff.
- **No pay-per-click trap** — The Ken is ad-free and subscription-only. Its revenue comes from readers, not advertisers — keeping editorial integrity intact.
- **Unique beats** — Covers areas most Indian business media ignores: climate-tech, biotech, science policy, edtech pedagogy, and the messy reality of Indian manufacturing.
- **Bite-sized + longform** — Morning newsletter (free) gives you a quick daily dose, while feature stories (paid) deliver 3,000-word masterclasses on a single subject.

The Ken has won multiple awards for its journalism and is widely regarded as the gold standard for Indian business storytelling. It's not for everyone — but if you want to _actually understand_ India's economy and tech landscape, there's no better read.
