**India, understood — one long conversation at a time.**

Hosted by writer, economist, and blogger Amit Varma, The Seen and The Unseen is widely regarded as India's most intellectually stimulating podcast. With a format that favors depth over brevity, each episode dissects a single topic — from the economics of Indian weddings to the philosophy of free speech — with guests who bring genuine expertise and contrarian thinking.

#### **Why This Podcast is a Masterclass in Curiosity**

- **Unmatched intellectual depth** — Episodes regularly run 2–3 hours, giving guests room to build arguments, share anecdotes, and challenge conventional wisdom without interruption.
- **Extraordinary roster of thinkers** — Economists (Karthik Muralidharan, Jean Drèze), historians (Ramachandra Guha, Vikram Sampath), journalists (Rohan Venkataramakrishnan), writers (Jerry Pinto, Annie Zaidi), and civil servants — all speaking with candor rarely heard in Indian public discourse.
- **The art of the conversation** — Amit Varma is a master interviewer who prepares exhaustively and listens intently. He doesn't just ask questions — he engages, challenges, and synthesizes ideas in real time.
- **Eclectic range of topics** — Urban planning, cricket statistics, constitutional law, linguistic history, nutrition science, meme culture, and the craft of writing — each treated with the same rigour and fascination.
- **Bonus episodes** — Solo episodes where Amit shares his own reading, thinking, and writing process — a masterclass in clear thinking for anyone who writes or creates.

For anyone who wants to understand India beyond the headlines — its contradictions, its complexities, and its quiet genius — The Seen and The Unseen is indispensable listening.
