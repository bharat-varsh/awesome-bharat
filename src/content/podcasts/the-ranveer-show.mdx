**India's biggest podcast — where curiosity meets conversation.**

The Ranveer Show (TRS) is India's #1 podcast across platforms, hosted by Ranveer Allahbadia — better known as BeerBiceps. What began as a fitness and lifestyle YouTube channel has evolved into a cultural phenomenon, with episodes spanning entrepreneurship, mental health, ancient Indian philosophy, sports psychology, and the untold stories of India's most extraordinary achievers.

#### **What Makes TRS Essential Listening**

- **India's most diverse guest list** — From Virat Kohli and MS Dhoni to Sadhguru and Sri Sri Ravi Shankar; from Shark Tank judges to Padma Shri scientists — no topic is off-limits and no guest is too big.
- **Deep, unfiltered conversations** — Episodes run 90–180 minutes, allowing guests to speak freely without soundbites. The result is raw, vulnerable, and often surprising storytelling.
- **Bilingual appeal** — Ranveer seamlessly switches between Hindi and English, making the podcast accessible to India's vast bilingual audience.
- **Consistent & prolific** — With 400+ episodes and multiple weekly drops, TRS has built a loyal following that tunes in for both entertainment and education.
- **Beyond the podcast** — The BeerBiceps ecosystem now includes TRS Clips, TRS Hindi, a fitness app, and a growing merchandise line.

The Ranveer Show has redefined what Indian podcasting can be — proving that longform, intelligent conversation can captivate millions in an age of short attention spans.
