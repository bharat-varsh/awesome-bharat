**Democratizing internal tooling — one open-source commit at a time.**

Navaneeth P.K. is an Indian entrepreneur and the co-founder & CEO of ToolJet, an open-source low-code platform that lets engineering teams build custom internal tools in minutes. Born and raised in Kerala, Navaneeth built ToolJet alongside co-founder Manish Kumar to address the inefficiencies he experienced while working as a software engineer — where teams spent weeks building simple admin panels and CRUD apps.

ToolJet has been adopted by thousands of companies globally and has over 15,000 GitHub stars, making it one of the most popular open-source projects to come out of India in recent years.
