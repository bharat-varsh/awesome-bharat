**Learn how the world works — one animated case study at a time.**

Think School, founded by Ganeshprasad, is one of India's fastest-growing educational YouTube channels. It uses gorgeous whiteboard animations and on-ground research to explain business models, government policies, geopolitical strategies, and economic trends — often featuring Indian examples that mainstream media overlooks.

#### **What Makes It Stand Out**

- **Deep-dive business case studies** — From Zomato's rise to Patanjali's strategy, every video is meticulously researched and visually mapped.
- **Geopolitics made simple** — Complex topics like the China-Taiwan conflict, RBI policy moves, or India's semiconductor mission are broken into digestible, engaging narratives.
- **Data-first storytelling** — Every claim is backed by charts, sources, and expert interviews — no fluff, no noise.

Whether you're a student, entrepreneur, or just curious about how money and power move in the real world, Think School offers a perspective that's rare in Indian YouTube — _analytical, objective, and visually stunning_.
