**Live the boAt life — where style meets sound.**

Founded in 2016 by Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta, boAt has rapidly grown from a niche audio brand into India's most-loved wearable and consumer electronics company. With a sharp focus on design, durability, and affordability, boAt has made premium audio and smart wearables accessible to the Indian masses.

#### **Why boAt Stands Out**

- **India's #1 audio & wearables brand** — boAt dominates the Indian market with over 30% share in hearables and a rapidly growing smartwatch lineup.
- **Designed for the Indian consumer** — From rugged Rockerz neckbands to the sleek Airdopes TWS earbuds, every product is built for Indian lifestyles — long battery life, IPX ratings for humidity, and bass-heavy sound signatures.
- **Celebrity-backed, youth-first** — Brand ambassadors like Ranveer Singh, Kriti Sanon, and Kiara Advani connect directly with Gen Z and millennial audiences.
- **Innovation at scale** — The boAt Lab in Bengaluru drives R&D for audio tuning, wearable sensors, and app integration — all in-house.

boAt has proved that an Indian brand can compete head-on with global giants like Apple, Samsung, and Sony — and win on the home turf. With over 100 million products shipped, it's not just a brand; it's a movement.
