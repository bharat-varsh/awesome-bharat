**Goodness inside. Goodness outside. Made with love for your family.**

Founded in 2016 by Varun Alagh and Ghazal Alagh, Mamaearth was born from a parent's concern — finding safe, natural, and effective products for their newborn. Today, it is one of India's fastest-growing personal care brands and a trailblazer in the toxin-free beauty movement.

#### **Why Mamaearth is a Game-Changer**

- **100% toxin-free promise** — Every product is free from parabens, sulfates, silicones, and artificial toxins. Mamaearth is India's first personal care brand with the global MadeSafe certification.
- **Innovative ingredients** — From Vitamin C daily glow face wash to Onion hair oil for hair regrowth, each product is built around a hero natural ingredient that delivers visible results.
- **Sustainability built-in** — Plastic-neutral operations, recyclable packaging, and a "Plant for Good" program that plants a tree for every order.
- **Baby care you can trust** — The Gentle Baby range is pediatrician-approved and free from 300+ harmful chemicals — giving new parents one less thing to worry about.

Mamaearth's meteoric rise — from a D2C startup to a publicly listed company with over ₹1,000 crore in revenue — proves that Indian consumers are hungry for products that are _safe, effective, and responsible_.
