India's AI story is one of **leapfrogging**. A country with 22 official languages, a massive smartphone-first population, and some of the world's best engineering talent is using AI not just to catch up — but to solve problems at planetary scale.

From Bhashini's real-time translation bridging the digital divide, to AI-powered crop disease detection saving millions of farmers, to Indian AI startups building foundational models for underserved languages — the opportunity is immense.

**Where you come in:** Whether you're a developer, researcher, student, or someone who just cares about India's tech future, there's a place for you in this journey. The actions above are just the beginning.
