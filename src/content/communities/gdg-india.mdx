**Where India's developers come together to learn, build, and lead.**

Google Developer Groups (GDG) India is a grassroots network of over 50 active chapters spanning every major Indian city — from Bengaluru to Bhopal, Chennai to Chandigarh. Each chapter is independently organized by local developer leaders, with support from Google, to create hyper-local learning experiences that are relevant, accessible, and free.

#### **What GDG India Offers**

- **Regular tech meetups** — Hands-on workshops, hackathons, codelabs, and tech talks covering Android, Flutter, TensorFlow, Google Cloud, Firebase, Kotlin, and more.
- **DevFest — India's largest developer conference** — Annual flagship event organized by GDG chapters across the country, featuring Google Developer Experts (GDEs), industry leaders, and hands-on sessions.
- **Google I/O Extended** — Local watch parties and satellite events that bring the global Google I/O experience to Indian developers.
- **Women Techmakers** — Dedicated programs and scholarships to support women in tech, including career workshops, mentorship circles, and leadership talks.
- **Student-focused programs** — Many GDG chapters run study jams, cloud bootcamps, and summer of code programs specifically for students and early-career developers.

GDG India is where India's developer community was born online and nurtured into a real-world movement. With thousands of events and millions of developer hours invested, it remains the single largest and most accessible tech community in the country.
