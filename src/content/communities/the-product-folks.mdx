**India's product community — united by curiosity, driven by impact.**

Founded in 2020 by Harsh Awasthi and Jivraj Singh Shekhawat, The Product Folks has rapidly grown into India's most vibrant professional community for product managers. With chapters in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, and beyond, TPF hosts regular meetups, workshops, and flagship events that bring together PMs from every stage — aspiring, early-career, and seasoned leaders.

#### **Why Join The Product Folks**

- **Community-first learning** — Weekly AMAs with product leaders from Google, Swiggy, Uber, CRED, and Zerodha; curated book clubs; and real-world case study discussions.
- **Mentorship programs** — Structured mentorship cycles connecting aspiring PMs with experienced product leaders across the industry.
- **Job & referral network** — An active #opportunities channel where members share openings, interview tips, and referral swaps — no recruiters, just peers.
- **Flagship events** — ProductCon, PM Summit, and chapter-specific meetups attract thousands of attendees and feature top voices in product thinking.
- **Curated resources** — Templates for PRDs, roadmaps, A/B testing frameworks, and a library of 100+ product teardowns contributed by the community.

TPF is free to join and open to all. Whether you're a seasoned PM or just curious about product as a career, you'll find your tribe here.
